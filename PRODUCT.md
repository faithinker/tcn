# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is international researchers, educators, and cultural or policy practitioners who need to understand TCN's purpose, governance, membership, and scholarly activity. Prospective members and event participants are a secondary audience. Authenticated TCN editors operate the supporting authoring and official Q&A surfaces.

## Product Purpose

TCN publishes its founding basis, people, scholarly activity, and official answers as a trustworthy English-language institutional record. Success means that a visitor can understand what TCN is, verify its activity and governance, and find the appropriate participation or contact path without additional explanation.

## Positioning

A durable institutional record for transcultural research and international scholarly exchange. TCN is not a social network, community feed, or general news publication.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Join / Contact.
- Secondary CTA: explore seminars or ask an official question.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: TCN is an international scholarly network for research and exchange across cultural boundaries.
- Belief ladder: TCN has a legitimate founding basis and operating principles; accountable people and official contact details; an ongoing record of scholarly activity; and a clear route to participate or ask a question.
- Proof on hand: the founding record, Declaration, Bylaws, member profiles, seminar archive, official answers, and Secretariat contact details.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, restrained, accountable. The interface should feel calm, exact, and institutional without using inflated marketing language or decorative signals of authority.

## Anti-references

- SaaS marketing landing pages.
- Social feeds and community apps.
- Link-dense news portals.
- Surfaces that make every link compete through blue text or persistent underlines.
- Repetitive cards, badges, and eyebrow labels used as template scaffolding.
- Decorative imitations of academic journals or newspapers.

## Design Principles

1. Official record before promotion.
2. Structure communicates hierarchy before decoration does.
3. Interactive elements stay clear without making every link compete.
4. Content truth and provenance take priority over conversion.
5. Public reading surfaces stay calm; administrative surfaces prioritise operational clarity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Support keyboard navigation and a clear `focus-visible` state.
- Do not rely on colour alone to identify inline links or current location.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep links embedded in prose distinguishable from surrounding text with a non-colour cue.
